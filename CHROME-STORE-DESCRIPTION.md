Dual Subtitles turns films and series into language practice.

It shows two subtitle lines while you watch: the original subtitle line and a
translation underneath it. Click any word in the subtitles to translate it, then
save useful words to your personal vocabulary book with the sentence they came
from.

The extension is free, requires no account, and is designed to keep your data
local whenever possible.

Supported websites:

- Amazon Prime Video
- Kino.pub

## Key features

**Dual subtitles**

Read the original line and a translated line at the same time. This helps you
connect what you hear with what it means without constantly pausing the video.

**Chrome built-in translation**

When Chrome's built-in Translator is available, translation is processed by
Chrome on your device. This is the default private mode and does not require an
API key from you or a paid account from the developer.

**One-click word translation**

Click a word in the subtitle overlay and a small translation popup appears. No
copy-paste, no extra tab, no interrupting the scene.

**Personal vocabulary book**

Save words you look up. Each entry keeps the word, translation, source language,
target language, translator, date, and the subtitle line where the word
appeared.

**Free online fallback**

If Chrome built-in translation is unavailable for your language pair, you can
explicitly choose a free online fallback: LibreTranslate or MyMemory. You can
also connect your own DeepL API key for higher-quality translations.

**Private by design**

Your settings and saved words are stored locally in your browser using
chrome.storage.local. Nothing is sent to the developer. Online translation
services receive only the word or subtitle line you choose to translate.

**Export your vocabulary**

Open the vocabulary page to search, filter, and export saved words as CSV or
JSON. CSV works well for spreadsheets and flashcard apps such as Anki.

**Full control panel**

Choose source and target languages, select the translator, turn the translated
line on or off, enable or disable click-to-translate, and adjust subtitle size,
position, and timing.

## How to use

1. Open a supported video page on Amazon Prime Video or Kino.pub.
2. Start playback with subtitles available.
3. Open the Dual Subtitles panel from the extension button or the on-page panel.
4. Choose your source and target languages.
5. Turn on the translated subtitle line.
6. Click words you want to understand.
7. Save useful words to your vocabulary book.

## Translation options

**Auto (Chrome, private)**

The default mode. It uses Chrome built-in translation when available. If the
language pair is not available, the extension asks you to choose an online
fallback or add an API key instead of silently sending text to an online service.

**Chrome built-in (free)**

Uses Chrome's built-in Translator directly. Availability depends on Chrome,
device support, and the selected language pair.

**Free online fallback**

Uses the no-key online fallback chain for quick translations. This may be less
private than Chrome built-in translation because the text being translated is
sent to the selected translation service.

**DeepL (API key)**

Uses your own DeepL API key. The key is stored locally in your browser and is
sent only to DeepL when translating.

**LibreTranslate / MyMemory**

No-key online translation options for quick lookups. Public free services may be
rate-limited or occasionally unavailable.

## Privacy

Dual Subtitles does not collect analytics, telemetry, or browsing history. It
does not have a backend server and does not send data to the developer.

When Chrome built-in translation is used, translation is processed locally by
Chrome. When an online translator is selected, only the text being translated is
sent to that selected service.

## What it does not do

Dual Subtitles does not download, record, copy, or redistribute video or audio.
It only reads subtitle text that the streaming page already provides and
re-renders it as clickable dual subtitles.
